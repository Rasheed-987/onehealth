Backing an EHS team at the ISBT transfusion congress

A new agreement brings Baxter's solutions to healthcare providers across the UAE, with local supply and service behind every order.

11 June 2026 2 min read

What the partnership covers

One Health and Baxter have signed a partnership to supply and support Baxter's solutions for healthcare providers across the UAE.

Under the agreement, One Health brings Baxter's portfolio to hospitals and clinics across the country, with local supply, integration, and service behind it. The specific products and terms come from the signed agreement.

This partnership puts proven therapies closer to the providers who need them, with local support behind every order.

The agreement adds to the portfolio of manufacturers One Health represents in the UAE.
