The supply chain that keeps healthcare stocked

We keep healthcare providers supplied, sourcing what they need and delivering it across the UAE and the GCC, in the right condition and on time. The same operation stores and distributes for manufacturers who need a partner in the UAE.

What we cover

Procurement

We source the products and services the business depends on through transparent and ethical purchasing, working with trusted suppliers for value without giving up quality or compliance.

Local and international sourcing
Supplier vetting and management
Speciality and hard to find items
Compliance and sustainability checks

Warehousing

We store each product to the conditions it needs and keep stock moving, so it is there when it is called for and none of it is lost to expiry.

Warehousing at KIZAD, Abu Dhabi
Controlled substance storage
Ambient storage
Real time inventory tracking
Cold chain storage held at 2 to 8 degrees
Stock rotation and expiry control

Logistics

We move goods through each stage of the chain to their destination, planned for on time delivery and tracked at every step.

Last mile across all seven emirates
Order tracking
Temperature controlled transport
Route planning and coordination

Handled to standard

The operation is certified to ISO 9001 for quality, ISO 14001 for the environment and ISO 45001 for health and safety.

Cold chain is monitored around the clock. Controlled substances are held under restricted access, and pharmaceutical stock is handled in line with good distribution practice. Every order can be traced at each step.
