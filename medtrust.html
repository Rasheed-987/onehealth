A One Health's brand

Medical consumables,
made to standard

MedTrust is our own UAE brand of medical consumables and infection control products, supplied to hospitals, governments, pharmacies, distributors and homecare providers across the UAE and the GCC.

Every product is sourced from a qualified manufacturer, released through a UAE quality team, and documented to the standard a tender asks for.

The range

Five families cover the consumables a healthcare facility gets through.

Personal protective equipment

Nitrile gloves, Type IIR masks, isolation gowns, PE aprons, caps and shoe covers.

Infection control

Sterile non woven swabs, antiseptic wipes, surface protection and sterilisation accessories.

Patient care

Wash basins, kidney dishes, examination table rolls, petroleum jelly and baby wipes.

Wound care

Sterile gauze and dressings, foam and hydrocolloid dressings, PE tape and plasters.

Homecare

Hygiene wipes, first aid consumables, incontinence support and patient comfort products.

Tender grade quality

Every product comes from an approved manufacturer, is checked before it ships, and is released by a UAE quality team before it reaches the market.

01

Approved manufacturer

Every product comes from a qualified, audited manufacturer. Imported lines carry CE marking and a complete technical file.

02

Pre-shipment check

Consignments are checked before they ship, with batch records and documentation verified against the order and the specification.

03

UAE quality release

A UAE quality team reviews and releases every product before it reaches the market, documented to the standard a tender asks for.

Making more of it here

Today the range is sourced from trusted manufacturers and quality checked before it reaches you.

MedTrust is built on a defined path to make more of it in the UAE, moving beyond sourcing into local assembly and, in time, full manufacturing.

It is a deliberate move in step with Make it in the Emirates and In Country Value, so more of what the country depends on is made closer to home.
