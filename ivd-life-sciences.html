Diagnostics & genomics
for the country's
laboratories

Diagnostics is the largest arm of One Health. The division covers the routine tests a hospital runs every day and the genome sequencing behind the national programme.

Every machine we install is backed by a UAE team that trains the people who use it and keeps it serviced, so the laboratory is supported here, not from a head office abroad.

Globally recognised brands

One Health represents the manufacturers behind the world's leading laboratories, many of them exclusively in the UAE, and brings their technology to hospitals and labs across the country.

Life sciences

Genomics and molecular science for clinical laboratories and the national programmes building the country's precision health capability.

Genomics

Population and clinical sequencing across targeted panels and whole genomes, with the bioinformatics to read them. This is part of the work supporting the Emirati Genome Programme.

Molecular diagnostics

PCR and syndromic panels, with the extraction systems behind them, for infectious disease and inherited conditions.

Microbiology

Rapid pathogen identification and antimicrobial susceptibility testing, so hospitals can act on resistance early.

Services & applications

One Health stays close to the laboratories it equips, long after installation.

Laboratory turnkey projects

Complete laboratory design and setup, including equipment planning and commissioning.

Training and technical support

Local specialists and service engineers who train your team and keep the equipment serviced.

Scientific support

A local team for assay validation and workflow tuning, on call when a method needs work.

Accreditation support

Products selected to align with JCI and CAP requirements, and a team that helps laboratories meet them.
