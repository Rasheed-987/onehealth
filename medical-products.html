The products and projects behind a working facility

We bring the medicines and medical equipment a hospital needs into the UAE, and plan and fit out the facilities that use them. A manufacturer comes here to reach the market. A provider comes here to equip a single department or fit out a whole facility.

Selected projects

Abu Dhabi

SEHA Saadiyat Clinic

A premium outpatient clinic on Saadiyat Island, equipped and commissioned as a turnkey project.

Abu Dhabi

SSMC Pura Longevity Clinic

A hyperbaric oxygen therapy chamber installed and commissioned for the longevity clinic at Sheikh Shakhbout Medical City.

Abu Dhabi

SEHA mobile clinics and screening buses

Two purpose built mobile units, a diagnostic clinic and a screening bus with an onboard digital X ray and laboratory, extending screening into the community.

Abu Dhabi

SAKINA for Children

A purpose built paediatric mental health clinic, equipped with medical and occupational therapy systems for assessment and treatment.

Alongside these, we have installed a cath lab and MRI at Sheikh Khalifa Specialty Hospital in Ras Al Khaimah, CT at Sheikh Khalifa Medical City Ajman and a LINAC radiotherapy system at Tawam, with further turnkey projects internationally in Syria, Seychelles and Ukraine.
