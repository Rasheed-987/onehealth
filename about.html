A single partner across the lifecycle

Most of the work begins after delivery.

The divisions source and supply the equipment, medicines, and consumables a facility needs, while also planning and fitting out the clinical spaces they go into.

They then maintain everything they install throughout its working life, with a single partner accountable for the entire process.
