Early careers & Emiratisation

We run a biomedical internship with local universities, a twelve month placement that gives early career engineers time on live hospital equipment, with a route to a permanent engineering role. It is part of how OneHealth builds UAE national talent in healthcare technology.

What you will do:

  • You will spend twelve months alongside our engineers on live hospital equipment.
  • You will rotate through planned maintenance, fault finding and installation work.
  • You will keep the service records for the assets you work on, to the same standard as the team.
  • You will finish with a portfolio of the equipment and procedures you have worked on.

Requirements:

  • You are a UAE national in the final year of, or recently graduated from, a biomedical or electrical engineering programme.
  • You are studying at, or have graduated from, one of our partner universities.
  • You are available for the full twelve months, based in Abu Dhabi.

What you’ll get:

  • Mentoring from the engineers who maintain the equipment day to day.
  • Manufacturer training on the systems you are placed with.
  • A route to a permanent engineering role at the end of the placement.

Placement:

Twelve months, paid
